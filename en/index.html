A WEEK IN MOSHI, A YEAR IN THE COMMUNITY

We support East Africa Cup

Superinvite contributes funds to East Africa Cup, an East African youth event that uses sport as a tool for development and safety.

The 2026 edition was held June 24–28 in Moshi, Tanzania, with eighteen football teams, sixty chess players, and a broad program of community-focused theme sessions.

A new digital registration platform and a strengthened coaching program, supported by UNESCO, boosted the work on safety and quality. The coaches are followed up until 2027, aiming for the Africa Championship – which will then be held in East Africa for the first time.

For us, it's a given to support organizers who create something that lasts long after the final match.
