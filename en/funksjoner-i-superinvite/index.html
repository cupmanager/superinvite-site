Features in Superinvite

Since 2012 we have worked with over 1100 clubs and associations in Norway, and created a leading solution for payments to clubs and associations.

Most of the features you'll find in Superinvite have been developed based on requests from our customers. This makes Superinvite a solid solution, tailored to the needs of clubs. Superinvite is a great fit for FFO (after-school football program), academies, football schools, player fees, or organizing volunteer work.

Get paid for what you want

With Superinvite you can easily get paid for what you want, and we support a wide range of features so you can structure this in a simple way. Whether you run the club's FFO / academy, or work with tournaments or football schools, Superinvite makes your everyday life easier.

Installment payments and recurring payments

In many cases it can be an advantage for members to split the amount to be paid into installment payments. It can also save time for all parties to charge a fixed amount per month, for example for FFO / academy, instead of paying once. Superinvite is Norway's leading provider of a fully digital installment payment and recurring payment solution.

Read more here →

Real-time lists and reports

In a modern digital world, we live more and more in real time when it comes to information. Superinvite has always focused on keeping both payments and lists up to date, so you have good control the day you need it. We solve this elegantly, and offer several options for the lists and reports you need in everyday life.

Read more here →

Communication with members

In Superinvite it's easy to communicate with members through a range of different options. You can invite via email, send payment reminders for unanswered invitations, send out messages, and more. At the start of 2020, we're also launching a brand new communication solution, where members can notify the club of their attendance status for invitations.

Read more here →

Finances and settlement

Clubs and associations should spend their time on children and young people, and as little time as possible on administration. We've worked on this since we started, saving clubs unnecessary resource use every day. Since every step happens digitally in real time, you get both settlement and lists updated the instant someone pays. Accounting records for your accountant are easy to send by generating a dedicated report designed for exactly that.
