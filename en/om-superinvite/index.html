About SuperInvite

Superinvite is owned by Invite Sports and is a solution that has existed since 2012. The solution is built by and for sports clubs in Norway and Sweden based on their needs. Since launch, over 1,100 clubs have been introduced to Superinvite. Together with Cup Manager, we form a complete solution in areas covering payments to clubs and associations, as well as the world's leading tournament solution. Our focus is to create a solution that works in the everyday life of clubs and associations.
