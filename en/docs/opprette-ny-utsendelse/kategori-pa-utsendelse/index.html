Category on invitation

If you work with a lot of different invitations, it can be a good idea to categorize them. This lets you simplify the overview of all invitations in your dashboard, and choose to show invitations for just one specific category. For example Training fees, FFO (after-school football program), Football schools – or if a club runs several sports, you can categorize by Football, Handball, Basketball, Skiing, etc. This is not a required field, and can be ignored if you're a smaller club.

You set up categories under "Settings", and "Manage categories".
